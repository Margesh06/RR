      1Find the pressure balls

      For every ball since 2022 we estimate each team's chance of winning. A pressure ball is one where the next delivery can swing that chance a lot.

      2Compare with an average player

      We compare what each player did with what an average player would have done in exactly the same situation, and with his own normal level.

      3Check it's skill, not luck

      We only trust a measure that gives the same answer on two random halves of a player's matches.

      4Test 288 versions

      We changed every judgement call and averaged the results, so no single choice decides the list.

      Is it skill or luck?

      How well each measure agrees with itself across two random halves of a player's matches (1 = always, 0 = pure luck).

      For batters, the usual "clutch" stat is almost pure luck (0.05); ours reaches 0.51. For bowlers, ours is about twice as reliable.

      Does mixing IPL and T20Is change the answer?

      We rated everyone again on IPL games only, then on T20Is only.

      The order barely changes. Using both mainly gives us more evidence per player.

      Is this better than the usual approach?

      Most analyses call the death overs "pressure" and rank by strike rate or economy, or by "under pressure minus normal". We tested all three on the same players.

      In plain words: the usual "compared with his normal level" approach is pure noise for bowlers and mostly rewards finishers for batters. Ours is the only one that makes that comparison while staying reliable and fair. Plain economy looks stable for bowlers mainly because it punishes anyone who bowls at the death.

      How to read this page

      Two minutes. No statistics needed.

      1. Pressure = the moments when the next ball could swing the match. About 1 ball in 5.
      2. "vs an average player" = what he did compared with what an average player would have done in exactly the same situation. This keeps openers and finishers fair.
      3. Delivered = in how many of his tense games he beat that average.
      4. How sure = we ran the analysis 73 ways. "Very sure" means he is top 10 in almost all of them.
      5. For Rajasthan Royals turns it into extra wins per season for RR.
      6. Anywhere on the page, hover or tap ⓘ to see what a term means, and hover a number for details.
